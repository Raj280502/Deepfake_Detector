What are DeepFakes?

DeepFakes are synthetic media created using deep learning techniques, typically involving face swapping or face generation. These manipulated videos and images can be used for:

  • Spreading misinformation and fake news
  • Identity theft and fraud
  • Harassment and reputation damage
  • Political manipulation

Our tool helps identify these manipulations by analyzing subtle artifacts and inconsistencies that deep learning models leave behind.
